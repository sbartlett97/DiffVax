Research Question

How can the DiffVax image immunization framework be adapted to operate effectively at 1088×1088 resolution and disrupt SOTA transformer-based diffusion models (DALL-E 3, Flux 1.x, SD3, SD3.5), given the current system is only proven at 512×512 against UNet-based diffusers?

Current state: Bootstrap complete. 26-paper literature survey done. 5 experiment protocols locked and committed. 4 code changes implemented and committed. Ready for training runs.

Key Findings from Literature (26 Papers)

The literature survey identified 7 actionable patterns directly applicable to DiffVax scaling.

P1 🔴 Critical: DiT models require middle-block disruption, not encoder attacks

DeContext (arXiv:2512.16625, Dec 2024) showed that contextual information in FLUX/SD3 editing flows primarily through middle transformer blocks during early denoising timesteps (t > 0.7) — not through the VAE encoder. Prior encoder attacks (PhotoGuard-style) lose significant effectiveness on DiT models for this reason.

DiffVax's Phase 7 was hooking early blocks — mechanistically wrong for DiT protection. FIXED in H1

P2 🔴 Critical: 18k-token gradient variance causes instability at 1088px

TGR (arXiv:2303.15754, CVPR 2023) showed high token-to-token gradient variance in ViT/DiT attention blocks is the primary cause of poor adversarial transfer. At 1088px, SD3.5's MM-DiT joint attention processes ~18,496 tokens — gradient variance is extreme.

Token-wise gradient normalization reduces variance and improves transfer by ~8.8%. FIXED in H4

P3 🟡 High Impact: Partial-timestep gradient = 50% VRAM reduction, same quality

"Distraction Is All You Need" (CVPR 2024) demonstrated that computing gradients only at a subset of critical timesteps achieves equivalent protection efficacy at ~50% VRAM cost. This directly addresses the 26-28 GB bottleneck preventing SD3.5 training at 1088px on 24 GB GPUs.

P4 🟡 Medium Impact: CLIP-H/14 provides model-agnostic DALL-E 3 protection

DALL-E 3 uses CLIP ViT-H/14 as its image conditioning backbone. Three independent papers (Glaze, DTIA, NL Adversarial) confirm that disrupting CLIP-H embeddings provides black-box transfer to DALL-E 3 without direct model access. DiffVax v2 uses ViT-B/32 — upgrading to ViT-H/14 covers DALL-E 3. Enabled via H3 config

P5-P7 Additional Insights

  • P5 (DDAP/AdvAD): High-frequency perturbation concentration improves imperceptibility at same L∞ budget — relevant for 1088px visual quality
  • P6 (Mist): Zero (black) loss target is suboptimal — DiT models have strong semantic priors to recover from black outputs. Random-sign noise target forces harder disruption. FIXED in H7
  • P7 (Hönig et al. ICLR2025): Pixel-space perturbations defeated by averaging 50+ images. DiffVax's content-conditioned NestedUNet is more resistant than fixed UAPs (Glaze, Mist) but still vulnerable. Scope protection claims to <10 images per subject.

Threat Model Note

Known Limitation: Robust Mimicry (Hönig et al., ICLR 2025)

Pixel-space perturbations can be defeated by an attacker who averages 50+ protected images of the same subject. DiffVax's content-conditioned NestedUNet is more resistant than fixed UAPs (Glaze, Mist) because perturbations vary with image content, but the fundamental vulnerability remains.

Recommendation: Scope protection claims to single-image or small-set (<10 images) attack scenarios in the paper. This is the appropriate threat model for social-media upload protection where most users upload limited content.
