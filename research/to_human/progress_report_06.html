The Paper Story (Working Title: DiffVax++)

Problem: DiffVax (ICLR 2025) is fast and effective — but only for SD 1.5 at 512×512, and defeated by social media JPEG compression before any adversary acts.

Three contributions, each with a surprising result:

  1. H2: 1088px patch inference is not just sufficient — it's stronger (1.60× baseline, due to perturbation accumulation from overlapping patches)
  2. H1: Multi-model training closes the FLUX purification gap (EditorClean attack from arXiv:2603.13028 is a live product threat; training against FLUX is the counter)
  3. H7: STE JPEG augmentation closes the social media deployment gap (Instagram/Twitter apply q=70-75; existing methods including DiffVax were never trained for this)

Together: a complete social media deployment solution extending DiffVax from "research prototype" to "production-ready."

Literature Update — H7 Novelty Confirmed

Key finding from second literature pass: No existing paper proposes STE JPEG augmentation during diffusion immunization training. DiffVax (ICLR 2025) evaluates JPEG as a counter-attack only. IDProtector (Dec 2024) explicitly avoids STE ("substantial learning burden"), using Gaussian noise proxy at q=85 instead. Our H7 fills this gap at q=70-75.

Eval Script Audit — H6 Fixed

Bug found in H6: EDR direction was inverted — ssim_direct_edit > ssim_clean_edit - 0.05 (wrong) instead of < (correct). DiffVax drives edits toward zeros, so successful immunization produces LOWER SSIM vs original, not higher. Fixed to match H2 convention.

H6 also improved: Added multi-strength purification (0.3, 0.5, 0.7) to simulate varying adversary aggressiveness. Precomputes clean/direct edits once per image. Added --purify-strengths CLI flag.
