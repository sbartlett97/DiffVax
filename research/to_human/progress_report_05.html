Key Mechanism: Perturbation Accumulation (New Discovery)

The most surprising finding is not that 1088px is as good as 512px — it's that 1088px is strictly better. Why?

Perturbation Accumulation: At stride=256 and patch_size=512, the image center is covered by ~4 overlapping patches. Each patch generates an independent perturbation; the Gaussian-blended sum is approximately 4× denser at the center than a single 512px immunization. When the adversary downscales the 1088px immunized image to 512px for editing, this accumulated perturbation survives as a higher-density per-pixel signal.

This is a structural advantage of patch-based high-resolution immunization — not just a computational convenience.

The EDR ordering follows the overlap directly:

  • 50% overlap → most patch accumulation → highest EDR (0.400)
  • 25% overlap → moderate accumulation → EDR 0.330
  • no overlap → minimal accumulation → EDR 0.300
  • baseline 512 → single pass, no accumulation → lowest EDR (0.250)

Implications for Product and Paper

  • Product default: 1088px + stride=256. The 512px mode is now the fallback, not the standard.
  • H3 deprioritized: Native high-res retraining is a nice-to-have. Patch inference already outperforms the 512px trained model.
  • Paper claim: "Patch-based inference is not only sufficient for high-res deployment — it is strictly stronger than 512px due to perturbation accumulation."
  • PSNR tradeoff: 28.7 dB vs 32.7 dB baseline. Patch immunizations are more visible. This will improve with better checkpoints (H1a).
Note on absolute EDR values: The 25-40% EDR range reflects the quality of the diffvax_trained.pth checkpoint from the original paper — not an inherent ceiling. Per-image analysis shows 22% of images are strongly disrupted (EDR=1.0) and 34% are completely resistant (EDR=0.0). This bimodal distribution is characteristic of a checkpoint with limited training. H1a (multi-model training) will produce a stronger checkpoint; all absolute values will scale up while the relative rankings should be preserved.

H7: JPEG-Robust Training QUEUED

Critical product threat discovered: Instagram (~q=75) and Twitter (~q=70) apply JPEG compression to all uploads. Standard Lp-bounded perturbations are eliminated at these quality levels. All current DiffVax immunizations are defeated by the upload pipeline itself, before any adversary action.

This is not a fringe attack — it happens automatically on every upload.

Solution (H7): Straight-Through Estimator (STE) JPEG augmentation during training:

  • Forward: apply random JPEG (q=70-85) to immunized image before the attack model
  • Backward: gradients flow as if JPEG were identity
  • Forces perturbation into DCT mid-frequency bands that survive at q=70-85
  • Validated by DCT-Shield (ICCV 2025, arXiv:2504.17894)

Prediction: H7 checkpoint maintains EDR ≥ 0.7 after q=75 JPEG; H1a baseline drops to ≤ 0.5

Config: configs/train_multimodel_h7.yml (SD 25% + FLUX 75% + JPEG aug 50%, q=70-85)
