Why H1a Fails: Perturbation Weakening

H1a's PSNR is 2.1 dB higher than sd15_only — meaning it produces a weaker perturbation. The FLUX gradient signal during training is ~10× larger than SD 1.5 (FLUX Loss₁ ≈ 0.8–1.3 vs SD 1.5 Loss₁ ≈ 0.05–0.15). Rather than forming a curriculum, the competing objectives cause the NestedUNet to converge on more conservative (smaller) perturbations.

Surprising positive: sd15_only already achieves FLUX EDR=0.200 and SD3.5 EDR=0.140 without multi-model training. The shared VAE bottleneck mediates cross-architecture transfer automatically. Multi-model training is not needed — and actively hurts.

Mechanism Hypothesis

DCT block quantization artifacts (8×8-pixel boundaries from JPEG compression) create an additional adversarial signal specific to FLUX's patch-token architecture. FLUX tokenizes the image at patch level; DCT block boundaries align with patch boundaries and create discontinuities that compound with the immunization perturbation. SD 1.5's convolutional UNet is robust to this artifact — explaining why SD1.5 EDR is JPEG-invariant.

Implication for H7: H7's STE JPEG training can now be motivated as "exploiting the JPEG paradox" rather than "surviving JPEG." The goal shifts from "don't lose EDR" to "further amplify the DCT-artifact effect at q=70-75."
